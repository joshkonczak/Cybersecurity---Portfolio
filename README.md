### Reflection (Individual Game)

The Fall 2026 Individual Game was my first time really putting my skills to the test in a competition setting. Some of the categories I felt most confident in were Network Traffic Analysis, Cryptography, and Password Cracking which all line up with what I have practiced in previous courses such as SEC 401 and SEC 504. A couple categories that I struggled with were Scanning & Reconnaissance, and Web App Exploitation. Both of these subjects showed me where my skills were lacking and where I can spend more time practicing on. I finished 964th out of 5,959 people, putting me in the top 17% for this game. I scored an overall 79.5% accuracy with an overall 73.6% completion. One key takeaway for me in the individual game was spending too much time on a specific question or subject and not saving time for the other questions or modules which really showed in my overall completion. 

### Reflection (Team Game)

The Fall 2026 Team Game was the second part of the CTF I competed in. I enjoyed the team setting more than the individual game as we were able to collaborate and divide up sections based on people's strengths. I focused on Network Traffic Analysis and Cryptography which carried over as strengths for me from the Individual game. Our team finished 174th out of 3,041 teams with 71% accuracy and 94.6% completion overall. 

### Highlights

- Strongest Category: Network Traffic Analysis, scoring 100% accuracy.
- Was able to learn different ways to approach a problem, specifically, the methodology to a password cracking problem.
-  A final highlight for me would definitely be the team portion of the tournament and being able to bounce questions off other people in my team to see how they approach a problem.
